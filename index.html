How We Make Our Caramel Apples

Follow our recipe to make a tasty caramel apple. Edit the ingredients and steps to match your business’s recipe.

Ingredients

  • 6 medium apples
  • 1 package of soft caramels
  • 2 tablespoons of water (or as directed on the caramel package)
  • Wooden sticks
  • Optional toppings: chopped nuts, sprinkles, or mini chocolate chips

What You Need

A saucepan or microwave-safe bowl, spoon, baking tray, parchment paper, and adult supervision when using hot caramel.

Directions

  1. Wash the apples and dry them completely.
  2. Carefully remove the stems and insert a wooden stick into the top of each apple.
  3. Prepare the tray by lining it with parchment paper.
  4. Melt the caramels with water according to the package directions, stirring until smooth. Ask an adult to help with hot caramel.
  5. Dip each apple into the caramel and rotate it to coat the outside.
  6. Add your favorite toppings while the caramel is still soft.
  7. Place the apples on the prepared tray and let the caramel set before serving.
  8. Serve and enjoy! Follow safe food-handling practices and any required storage instructions.
